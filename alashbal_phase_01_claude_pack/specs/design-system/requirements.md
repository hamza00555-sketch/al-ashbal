# Design System — Requirements

## الهدف

إنشاء نظام تصميم أولي لتطبيق الأشبال.

## Required Components

- Button
- Card
- Badge
- ProgressBar
- ProgressRing
- StatCard
- Avatar
- SectionTitle
- PageHeader
- MobileNav
- DesktopSidebar

## Visual Rules

- Mostly purple.
- Limited white cards.
- Circular elements.
- Rounded corners.
- Soft glow.
- Arabic RTL.
- Baloo-like typography.

## States

- success
- warning
- danger
- pending
- neutral
